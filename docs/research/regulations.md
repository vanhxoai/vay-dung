## 0. Headline findings

| Topic | Finding |
|---|---|
| **DTI** | **No State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) debt-to-income cap exists for banks or finance companies** in any text I read. Lenders must only confirm "financial capacity to repay". See section 5. |
| Finance-company loan size | Total consumer-loan balance per customer at one finance company is capped at **100,000,000 VND** (Circular 43/2016, art. 3.1). A proposal to raise it to 400 million was still a proposal on 3 Oct 2026. |
| "400 million" | Circular 29/2026 raised the **"small-value loan" threshold** (simplified appraisal) to 400 million VND at credit institutions other than people's credit funds, effective 15 Aug 2026. This is **not** a lending cap and **not** the finance-company cap. Do not mix them up in copy or data. |
| Interest cap | The Civil Code 20%/year cap does **not** bind credit institutions (they are governed by their own law). It does bind non-bank lenders; interest of 100%/year or more is the criminal "loan-sharking" threshold. |
| Disclosure | Credit institutions must show an equivalent rate **in %/year, 365-day year, on actual outstanding balance** whenever the quoted rate is flat or not annual. Finance companies must publish their rate range, fees and interest method on their website. |
| Insurance | Law 32/2024 art. 15(5) **bans tying non-mandatory insurance to banking products "in any form"**. Our tool should flag bundled insurance. |
| Personal data | New regime since **1 Jan 2026** (Law 91/2025/QH15 + Decree 356/2025/NĐ-CP). Decree 13/2023 is repealed. Waitlist emails need specific, recorded consent. Using a **platform outside Vietnam** to process data collected in Vietnam is a "cross-border transfer" needing an impact-assessment dossier within 60 days. This is the most likely compliance gap for launch. |
| Selling data | Buying or selling personal data is **prohibited** (Law 91/2025 art. 7(6)). Any future "lead selling" that passes borrower contact details to lenders for money needs legal review before it is built. |
| Brokerage licence | I found **no** licence or registration requirement for a free, no-commission information/comparison site. I also found no explicit exemption. **UNCERTAIN**, lawyer to confirm. |

**Possible launch blockers (none is a confirmed prohibition):**
1. Cross-border transfer paperwork for the waitlist email provider, analytics and hosting (section 4.4).
2. Any analytics that records user behaviour may be "sensitive data" under Decree 356 art. 4(1)(l) (section 4.3).
3. Our own marketing: claims such as "rẻ nhất" need documentary proof (section 3).
4. Not a launch blocker but a roadmap blocker: commissions or lead selling (sections 2 and 4.7).

## 1. Rate and cost disclosure for consumer loans

### 1.1 Law on Credit Institutions (Luật Các tổ chức tín dụng) 32/2024/QH15

- **Issuer:** National Assembly (Quốc hội). **Passed:** 18 Jan 2024. **Effective:** 1 Jul 2024 (some provisions later).
- **Amended by:** Law 43/2024/QH15 (effective 1 Aug 2024) and Law 96/2025/QH15 (passed 27 Jun 2025, effective 15 Oct 2025).
- **Status:** In force. Replaced the 2010 Law. I read consolidated text 158/VBHN-VPQH (Sep 2025). A newer consolidation, 100/VBHN-VPQH (2026), exists per a search lead; I did not read it. **UNCERTAIN:** whether anything changed after Law 96/2025.
- **Relevant articles (read in full text):**
  - **Art. 100:** credit institutions must publicly post deposit rates and service fees; lending rates and credit fees are **agreed with the customer** under banking law (art. 100(2)). The Governor may set a rate mechanism in abnormal conditions (art. 100(3)).
  - **Art. 10(4):** credit institutions must publish deposit rates, service fees, and customers' rights and obligations for each product.
  - **Art. 15(5):** credit institutions and their staff must not tie the sale of **non-mandatory insurance** to banking products or services "in any form".
  - **Art. 15(2):** an organisation that is not a credit institution must not carry out banking activity (taking deposits, granting credit, providing payment accounts, art. 4(17)).
  - **Art. 5:** a non-credit institution must not use words such as "ngân hàng" or "công ty tài chính" in its name, titles, documents or advertising if this could mislead customers.
  - **Art. 102:** before granting credit the institution must obtain proof of financial capacity and a feasible plan, except small-value loans where it needs only minimum information on purpose and financial capacity (no ratio is set).
- **What it means for Vay Đúng:**
  - Do not use "ngân hàng", "tín dụng" or "công ty tài chính" in our name or brand claims (art. 5).
  - Never present ourselves as a lender or take applications (art. 15(2)).
  - Flag insurance sold with a loan as a warning, and show it as a separate line when optional (art. 15(5)). If a lender treats insurance as compulsory, record that as a contradiction to verify, not a fact.
- **Source:** https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2025/9/158-vbhn-vpqh.pdf (consolidated); original: https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?pageid=27160&docid=211190

### 1.2 Circular 39/2016/TT-NHNN on lending by credit institutions (banks and finance companies)

- **Issuer:** SBV Governor (Thống đốc NHNN). **Dated:** 30 Dec 2016. **Effective:** 15 Mar 2017.
- **Amendments (all in the official consolidation 86/VBHN-NHNN, signed 30 Jun 2026):**
  - 06/2023/TT-NHNN (28 Jun 2023, effective 1 Sep 2023): added electronic lending.
  - 10/2023/TT-NHNN (23 Aug 2023): suspended some of 06/2023's content.
  - 12/2024/TT-NHNN (28 Jun 2024, effective 1 Jul 2024): introduced "small-value loan" and removed the feasible-plan requirement for them.
  - 52/2025/TT-NHNN (25 Dec 2025): updates SBV unit names after the SBV reorganisation (Decree 26/2025/NĐ-CP).
  - 29/2026/TT-NHNN (30 Jun 2026, effective 15 Aug 2026): small-value ceiling raised; overdue-debt order; electronic-lending changes.
- **Status:** **In force and not replaced.** The consolidated text still cites the 2010 Law in its preamble; I did not find a replacement circular aligned with the 2024 Law. **UNCERTAIN:** whether SBV has a full replacement in preparation.
- **Articles that matter (read in full text):**
  - **Art. 13(1):** rates are agreed by supply and demand; SBV may cap only certain short-term VND loans for priority sectors (art. 13(2)), which are not consumer loans.
  - **Art. 13(3):** the loan agreement must state the rate **and the interest calculation method**. If the rate is **not expressed in %/year** and/or **not computed on the actual outstanding balance**, the agreement must also state the **equivalent rate in %/year (365-day year) on actual outstanding balance and time**.
  - **Art. 13(4):** late-payment interest on overdue interest is capped at **10%/year of the overdue interest**; overdue principal may bear at most **150% of the in-term rate**.
  - **Art. 16(1):** before the agreement, the lender must tell the customer the interest rate, overdue and late rates, the calculation method, and the **types and amounts of fees**.
  - **Art. 14 and 18(2):** prepayment fee and prepayment terms are by agreement.
  - **Art. 18(4) (as amended by 29/2026):** for overdue debt, principal is collected before interest.
  - **Art. 23(4):** standard-form contracts and general conditions must be posted at the office and on the lender's website, and given to the customer before signing with the customer's confirmation.
  - **Art. 7(4):** the borrower needs "financial capacity to repay". No ratio.
  - **Art. 2(13) (as amended by 29/2026):** small-value loan = a loan under Law art. 102(2) not exceeding **200 million VND at people's credit funds and 400 million VND at other credit institutions**. This sets only the level of documentation required.
  - **Art. 32a-32e:** electronic lending (information-security level 3 or above, KYC rules, e-contract). The old fixed 100 million electronic-balance limit (former art. 32c) is **repealed**; each lender now sets its own per-customer limit (secondary source for the last point).
- **What it means for Vay Đúng:**
  - The legally defined comparable rate is the **%/year, reducing-balance, 365-day** equivalent. This is exactly what our cost engine should show first, and we should label it clearly when a lender gives only a flat monthly rate.
  - Our IRR/APR includes fees and insurance, which the legal "equivalent rate" does not. Label ours as a **Vay Đúng estimate of total cost**, not "the legal rate".
  - Late-payment caps (10%/year; 150%) let us sanity-check published penalty terms. If a published penalty exceeds them, record a **contradiction** and do not list it silently.
- **Source:** https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2026/7/86-vbhn-nhnn.pdf

### 1.3 Circular 43/2016/TT-NHNN on consumer lending by finance companies, as amended by Circular 18/2019/TT-NHNN

- **Issuer:** SBV Governor. **43/2016:** dated 30 Dec 2016, effective 15 Mar 2017. **18/2019:** dated 4 Nov 2019, effective 1 Jan 2020.
- **Status:** **In force** per all sources I found. vbpl.vn marks Circular 43 "hết hiệu lực một phần" (partly expired). **UNCERTAIN: which parts.** I found no 2026 replacement. SBV circulated a draft amendment (Official Letter 9294/NHNN-CSTT, 23 Oct 2025, 12 articles) on 100 million to 400 million; a press article of 3 Oct 2026 still called the raise "a proposal, not a rule in force".
- **Articles (read in full text):**
  - **Art. 3(1):** consumer loan = VND loan to an individual for goods/services for personal or family consumption, with **total consumer-loan balance per customer at that finance company not exceeding 100,000,000 VND**. The cap does not apply to a car loan secured by that car.
  - **Art. 3(2) (as amended):** covers vehicles, household goods, education, medical, travel, culture, sport, home repair.
  - **Art. 4 (as amended):** matters not covered follow the general lending circular (Circular 39).
  - **Art. 9:** each finance company must set a system-wide **rate range (min and max) for each consumer product** and report it to SBV within 10 working days of any change.
  - **Art. 10(1)(e):** the contract must state the agreed rate **and the %/year equivalent (365 days, actual balance)**, the method, and the types and amounts of fees. Art. 10(1)(i): prepayment terms and fee.
  - **Art. 10a (added by 18/2019):** finance companies must **post and publish on their website** the rate range, all fees and the interest method; contact details; list of service points; basic customer rights and duties; FAQs. Complaints must be answered within 48 hours for money, rate or collection disputes, and within 7 working days for others.
  - **Art. 7(2)(đ) (as amended):** collection must contain no threats; **at most 5 reminders per day, only between 07:00 and 21:00**; no contacting people who do not owe the debt.
  - **Art. 8a (added by 18/2019):** direct disbursement (cash) only to customers **without bad debt on the CIC credit report**; the share of direct-disbursed consumer loans in the company's consumer credit balance must be at most **30% from 1 Jan 2024**; counted only for customers with direct-disbursed balance above 20,000,000 VND.
- **What it means for Vay Đúng:**
  - For finance-company products, the website rate range, fee table and method (art. 10a) are the **primary source** to use for `sourceUrl`.
  - Finance-company products with "from X%" ranges are legally **ranges**, so use `confidence: medium` and show the range as a range.
  - Respect the 100 million cap in the **eligibility screening** ("this finance company cannot lend you more than 100 million in total"), and keep it in data as a sourced field with an `asOf` date, since the raise to 400 million may become law.
- **Sources:** https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/van-ban/thong-tu-so-43-2016-tt-nhnn-22261.htm ; https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/van-ban/thong-tu-so-18-2019-tt-nhnn-29996.htm ; status page https://vbpl.vn/nganhangnhanuoc/Pages/vbpq-thuoctinh.aspx?ItemID=117498 ; draft (secondary) https://vnba.org.vn/vi/vnba-lay-y-kien-hoi-vien-ve-du-thao-sua-doi--bo-sung-thong-tu-43-19453.htm ; 3 Oct 2026 status (secondary) https://doanhnghiephoinhap.vn/vay-tieu-dung-co-nen-nang-tran-100-trieu-dong-150592.html

### 1.4 Circular 29/2026/TT-NHNN (new in 2026)

- **Issuer:** SBV Governor. **Dated:** 30 Jun 2026. **Effective:** 15 Aug 2026 (date confirmed by the official consolidation 86/VBHN-NHNN). Contracts signed before 15 Aug 2026 continue under the old terms (secondary source).

### 1.5 Interest-rate cap: Civil Code art. 468 and credit institutions

- **Civil Code (Bộ luật Dân sự) 91/2015/QH13, art. 468(1):** agreed interest must not exceed **20%/year** of the loan "unless another relevant law provides otherwise"; the excess is void. (Secondary sources and my reading; **I did not fetch the official text**, so confidence is medium.) Effective 1 Jan 2017.
- **Does it apply to credit institutions? No, in practice.** The "other law" is the Law on Credit Institutions art. 100(2) and Circular 39 art. 13(1), which let credit institutions agree rates by market supply and demand. A Ministry of Justice representative was reported in 2023 as saying that no specific rate ceiling exists for consumer lending by finance companies (secondary; https://div.gov.vn/lai-suat-trong-bo-luat-dan-su-2015-gop-phan-bai-tru-tin-dung-den).
- **Non-banks:** the 20% cap applies. Under Resolution 01/2021/NQ-HĐTP, "loan-sharking" (Criminal Code 2015, art. 201) means interest of at least 5 times the 20% ceiling, i.e. **100%/year or more** (secondary). **UNCERTAIN:** whether Criminal Code amendments in 2025 changed art. 201; I could not confirm.
- **What it means for Vay Đúng:**
  - Never tell users "legal maximum is 20%" for banks or finance companies. That is wrong and could mislead.
  - A lender not on the SBV licensed list with a rate above 20%/year is a strong scam signal. Above 100%/year it is potentially criminal. This supports the "tín dụng đen" warning content.
  - Use "no legal cap for licensed lenders, so compare" as the honest message.

## 2. Loan intermediaries, brokers, comparison sites, agents

- **What the law says (read in full text):**
  - Law 32/2024 art. 4(17): "banking activity" is deposit-taking, **granting credit**, and payment accounts. Introducing or comparing loans is not on this list.
  - Art. 15(2) bans non-credit-institutions from banking activity. A comparison tool that does not lend does not breach it.
  - Art. 4(20) "môi giới tiền tệ" (money brokerage) means fee-based arrangement of banking transactions **between credit institutions**. It does not cover introducing consumers.
  - Art. 113: commercial banks may appoint and act as **agents** in banking activity and payment agency "under Governor rules"; Circular 43 art. 6 lets a finance company run its own "điểm giới thiệu dịch vụ" (service introduction points), which are the finance company's own units reporting to SBV.
- **What I did not find:** any statute or SBV rule that requires a licence or registration for (a) publishing comparisons or (b) introducing borrowers to lenders. I also found no rule that expressly exempts a no-commission tool. One lawyer FAQ (iLAW) claims loan brokerage is a credit-institution function; it relies on the repealed 2010 Law. **UNCERTAIN.**
- **Where a free tool could cross a line (flag to lawyer):**
  1. **Taking commissions or per-lead fees** from lenders: this may make us an agent or broker. The agent regime for finance companies is **UNCERTAIN** (I did not locate an SBV circular on agents for non-bank lenders).
  2. **Collecting application data and passing it to lenders** (disclosure of financial information, personal-data rules in section 4).
  3. **Charging borrowers** any fee for access to loans (looks like advance-fee fraud; also a banned data/credit service risk).
  4. **Matching lenders and investors** (P2P). P2P is only allowed under SBV's controlled sandbox (Decree 94/2025/NĐ-CP, effective 1 Jul 2025, secondary source) for participants with an SBV certificate.
- **What it means for Vay Đúng:** MVP as described (static data, browser calculations, no commissions, no applications, waitlist email only) looks outside every regulated activity I found. Keep it that way: outbound links to the lender's own page, no pre-filled application, no referral fee, wording that we are "thông tin tham khảo, không phải tư vấn tín dụng hay môi giới".
- **Sources:** https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2025/9/158-vbhn-vpqh.pdf ; Decree 94/2025 (secondary) https://div.gov.vn/chinh-phu-cho-phep-thu-nghiem-fintech-co-kiem-soat-trong-linh-vuc-ngan-hang-tu-1-7-2025

## 3. Advertising credit products, comparisons, naming lenders

- **Law on Advertising (Luật Quảng cáo) 16/2012/QH13**, amended by 35/2018/QH14, 42/2024/QH15, 47/2024/QH15 and **75/2025/QH15 (passed 16 Jun 2025, effective 1 Jan 2026)**. Consolidated as 88/VBHN-VPQH (22 Aug 2025). **In force.**
- **Guidance:** **Decree 342/2025/NĐ-CP (effective 15 Feb 2026)** replaced Decree 181/2013/NĐ-CP and Decree 70/2021/NĐ-CP, and **Circular 12/2026/TT-BVHTTDL (22 May 2026, effective 5 Jul 2026)**. Both are known from **secondary sources only**. I did not read their text.
- **Articles read in full (Law 75/2025):**
  - **Art. 2(1) as amended:** "advertising" now means using people or media to introduce products, goods, services or the organisations that provide them. The earlier definition's profit-making test is gone. **UNCERTAIN:** whether a neutral, non-profit comparison tool that names lenders could be treated as "advertising" and so fall under the Advertising Law. No guidance found.
  - **Art. 8(10) as amended:** a prohibited act is "advertising that compares your own products or services with similar products or services of others **without lawful supporting documents**". Comparison is therefore allowed if documented.
  - New rules cover influencers/endorsers ("người chuyển tải sản phẩm quảng cáo"), cross-border online advertising, and online ad controls from 1 Jan 2026 (secondary).
- **Credit-specific advertising rules:** I found **none** in the Advertising Law or in SBV texts I read. Finance companies must publish rate ranges and fees (Circular 43 art. 10a); that is a disclosure duty on lenders, not a restriction on third parties. **UNCERTAIN:** whether Decree 342 or an SBV rule adds credit-ad requirements (for example mandatory warnings). I did not read Decree 342.
- **Naming lenders:** no rule found that forbids naming licensed lenders or comparing their published terms. Using **logos** raises trademark/IP questions that I did not research. Recommend text names only until a lawyer confirms. Law 32/2024 art. 5 governs only what words we may use for ourselves.
- **Consumer Protection Law 19/2023/QH15** (effective 1 Jul 2024) and Decree 55/2024/NĐ-CP: duties on traders to give full, accurate information and fair standard-form terms. I did not read them; whether they reach an information site is **UNCERTAIN**.
- **What it means for Vay Đúng:**
  - Our **own** marketing (social ads, influencer posts, landing page) is regulated advertising. No "rẻ nhất", "lãi suất thấp nhất", "duyệt 100%" without documentary proof dated and sourced; keep the evidence file (the `sourceUrl` + `retrievedAt` data does this).
  - Show **retrieval date** next to every number and call the ranking "xếp hạng theo chi phí thực tế ước tính" with method link.
  - Any influencer or affiliate promoting us must disclose the relationship.
- **Sources:** Law 75/2025 text: https://storage-vnportal.vnpt.vn/gov-hni/7218/VanBan/2025/7/30/LU%E1%BA%ACT%20S%E1%BB%AAA%20%C4%90%E1%BB%94I%20B%E1%BB%94%20SUNG%20LU%E1%BA%ACT%20QU%E1%BA%A2NG%20C%C3%81O.pdf (a provincial portal copy of the official text; the official consolidation is 88/VBHN-VPQH on vbpl.vn); Decree 342 (secondary) https://hcc.nghean.gov.vn/laws/detail/Nghi-dinh-so-342-2025-ND-CP-cua-Chinh-phu-Quy-dinh-chi-tiet-mot-so-dieu-cua-Luat-Quang-cao-2083/

## 4. Personal data protection

### 4.1 The current framework

- **Law on Personal Data Protection (Luật Bảo vệ dữ liệu cá nhân) 91/2025/QH15.** Issuer: National Assembly. Passed 26 Jun 2025. **Effective 1 Jan 2026** (art. 38). Source (Official Gazette no. 971+972): https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/van-ban/luat-so-91-2025-qh15-45578.htm
- **Decree 356/2025/NĐ-CP** (Government, 31 Dec 2025, **effective 1 Jan 2026**) details the Law. **Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP ceased to be in force on 1 Jan 2026** (Decree 356 art. 42(2)). Source (Gazette no. 18): https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/van-ban/nghi-dinh-so-356-2025-nd-cp-468371.htm
- **Status:** both in force. I found no later amendment. **UNCERTAIN:** there may be a penalty decree or further guidance issued after January 2026; I did not locate one.
- **Penalties (Law art. 8):** up to **3 billion VND** for ordinary violations; up to **5% of prior-year revenue** for unlawful cross-border transfer; for selling data, up to **10 times the revenue obtained** (or the 3 billion floor). Individuals pay at most half the organisation's maximum.

### 4.2 Consent for a waitlist email

- **Is an email address personal data?** Yes. It identifies or helps identify a person (Law art. 2(1)); Decree 356 art. 3(11) catch-all places it in "basic" data.
- **Law art. 9:** consent is valid only if voluntary and informed about (a) the type of data and purpose, (b) who controls it, (c) the person's rights and obligations. It must be expressed clearly, in a verifiable form (electronic is fine), **per purpose**, with **no bundling** of other purposes as a condition, and **silence is not consent**.
- **Decree 356 art. 6:** acceptable methods include a consent setting on a website or app; the controller must **store the consent record** (who, when, what was consented to), and **bears the burden of proof**. **No pre-ticked boxes or confusing defaults.**
- **Withdrawal (Law art. 10):** the person can withdraw consent in writing/electronically; you must act within the legal deadline (Decree 356 art. 5, which I did not read in full).
- **Marketing (Law art. 28):** using data for advertising needs consent that states content, form and frequency, a way to refuse, and compliance with anti-spam law. Tracking a visitor across the site for targeted advertising needs consent (art. 28(8)).
- **What it means for Vay Đúng (waitlist):**
  1. One unticked checkbox, text in Vietnamese: what we collect (email only), why (to tell you when Vay Đúng launches), who is the controller (name, contact), how long we keep it, how to withdraw. Link to the privacy notice.
  2. **Separate** checkbox for any other mailing (for example product news) so launch notice is not tied to it.
  3. Store: email, timestamp, consent text version, source page.
  4. Honour unsubscribe and delete requests.
  5. Do not buy or sell lists.

### 4.3 What counts as sensitive data; is income sensitive?

- **Decree 356 art. 4(1) (list read in full):** includes health, biometrics, precise location, ID card images, bank-account login and card data and transaction history, and (k) "**financial and credit information and records of financial transaction activity of a customer at credit institutions, payment intermediaries, securities, insurance and other permitted organisations**", and (l) **behavioural tracking data and usage of telecom, social networking and online services**.
- **Income typed into our calculator:** it is not named in the list. Item (k) is framed around data held at financial institutions. I could not confirm that a salary figure the user types in is sensitive. Press about the March 2025 draft said salary and allowances were added; **the final text I read does not list them**. **UNCERTAIN.** Safest approach: treat income and debts as sensitive-grade, and never receive them on our servers.
- **Behaviour/analytics:** item (l) is worded broadly. A conventional analytics tool that logs page views, clicks and device identifiers could arguably fall in. **UNCERTAIN** (no official guidance found). Prefer no analytics, or privacy-preserving, cookie-less, aggregate analytics with no persistent identifier.
- **Consequence of being sensitive:** consent must tell the person the data is sensitive (Decree 356 art. 6(4)); access limits and security rules (art. 4(2)); stricter handling for finance sector actors (Law art. 27, Decree art. 8: annual compliance check, full processing logs, 72-hour breach notice). **UNCERTAIN** whether Vay Đúng counts as "operating in the finance and banking sector" for these.

### 4.4 Cross-border transfer (US email provider, analytics, hosting)

- **Law art. 20(1):** a transfer includes (a) moving data stored in Vietnam to systems outside Vietnam, (b) giving data to a recipient abroad, and (c) **using a platform outside Vietnam to process personal data collected in Vietnam**. Art. 20(2)-(3): file a **cross-border impact assessment dossier** with the data-protection authority (Ministry of Public Security) within **60 days of the first transfer**; one dossier for the life of the organisation, updated when things change.
- **Exemptions (art. 20(6), Decree 356 art. 17(3)):** state bodies; employer storing its own staff data in the cloud; the data subject transferring their own data; journalism; data already lawfully public; emergencies; limited travel/payment contracts. **None fits a waitlist email list stored with a US vendor.**
- **Small business relief (Law art. 38(2)-(3); Decree art. 41):** small and start-up businesses may opt out of arts. 21, 22 and 33(2) (the **processing** impact assessment, its updates, and the data-protection officer requirement) for 5 years, and micro businesses are exempt, **unless** they process personal-data services, **directly process sensitive data**, or reach **100,000 data subjects**. **Art. 20 (the cross-border dossier) is not in that list**, so the relief may not cover the cross-border dossier. **UNCERTAIN** — lawyer to confirm.
- **Does an email list at Mailchimp/Resend/Vercel/Google Analytics trigger it?** On the text, yes under art. 20(1)(c). Visitor IP addresses logged by a foreign host may also count. There is no de minimis threshold.
- **Options for the MVP (decision for the team and lawyer):**
  1. Use a processor with servers and contract in Vietnam for the waitlist.
  2. Or accept the paperwork: file the dossier within 60 days of the first email stored abroad.
  3. Or do not collect emails until the lawyer confirms (the waitlist is the only server-side personal data in the MVP).
  4. Sign a data-processing contract with any processor (Law art. 37(2); Decree art. 12 for cloud services).
  5. Minimise: email only, no names or phone numbers.

### 4.5 Privacy notice contents

The Law requires informed consent (art. 9(2)) and gives data subjects rights to know, withdraw, view, correct, delete, restrict, object, complain and claim damages (art. 4). Finance-sector controllers must also state, when asking consent: purposes (including credit scoring if any), data sources and sharing parties, retention period, and how to withdraw and how deletion works (Decree 356 art. 8(2)). A good notice therefore states: controller identity and contact; data collected (email only); purpose; retention period; processors and **countries** where data is stored; rights and how to use them; no sale or sharing with lenders; cookies/analytics; date of version.

### 4.6 Is processing income only in the user's browser "processing by us"?

- **Law art. 2(6)** defines processing as collecting, analysing, storing, transferring etc. A **controller** is whoever decides the purposes and means (art. 2(7)).
- **Reasoned view, not official guidance:** if the data is typed into a page, calculated by JavaScript running on the user's device, and **never transmitted to or logged by us or our vendors**, we do not "collect" it, so the data-protection duties for that data should not arise. Keeping the calculator's code open and describing the behaviour in the notice supports this.
- **Risks that break the argument:** error-reporting tools capturing form fields; analytics events that include chosen amounts or income bands; session replay; server-side rendering of results; sharing results by link containing numbers in the URL (the URL can be logged); saving to a cloud account. **UNCERTAIN:** no regulator has addressed local-only processing. Lawyer to confirm. Design rule: **no network request may contain any value the user typed into the calculator.**

### 4.7 Selling or sharing data

**Law art. 7(6) prohibits buying and selling personal data** except where a law allows it, and art. 8(3) sets a fine of up to 10 times the revenue obtained. Passing a borrower's contact details to lenders for payment is the classic lead-selling model. It is not in the MVP, but it is a **legal risk for the business model**, not just an ethics choice. Ask the lawyer whether a consented, user-initiated referral could be lawful before ever building it.

## 5. Debt-to-income (DTI) guidance and loan limits

**Answer: there is no SBV-imposed or SBV-recommended DTI/DSR cap for consumer loans, at banks or at finance companies, in any text I read.**

What the law requires instead:

| Rule | Exact content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Ability to repay | Borrower must have "financial capacity to repay" (khả năng tài chính để trả nợ). No ratio, no percentage. | Circular 39 art. 7(4), full text |
| Appraisal | Lender must obtain documents proving financial capacity, except for small-value loans where it needs only "minimum information" on purpose and financial capacity. | Law 32/2024 art. 102(1)-(2) |
| Small-value threshold | Up to **400,000,000 VND** at credit institutions other than people's credit funds (**200,000,000** at people's credit funds), effective **15 Aug 2026**. Before that: 100,000,000 VND (Circular 12/2024, effective 1 Jul 2024). **This is a documentation threshold, not a lending cap.** | Circular 39 art. 2(13) as consolidated in 86/VBHN-NHNN |
| Finance-company loan size | Total consumer-loan balance per customer at one finance company: **at most 100,000,000 VND**, except a car loan secured on that car. | Circular 43 art. 3(1) |
| Finance-company cash loans | Direct-disbursed (cash) loans only to customers without bad debt on the CIC; direct-disbursed loans at most **30%** of the company's consumer credit balance (from 1 Jan 2024), counted for customers with more than 20 million VND directly disbursed. | Circular 43 art. 8a (added by 18/2019) |
| Number of loans per borrower | **No rule found** limiting the number of loans, or total debt across lenders. | Reading of Circulars 39, 43, 18/2019 and Law 32/2024 |
| 2025-26 proposals | Draft amending Circular 43 (Oct 2025): 100 million to 400 million (press does not mention DTI). A 2 Oct 2026 workshop said any raise should come with tight control of the "debt obligation to income ratio" and better CIC data. These are **proposals**, not rules. | VNBA 1 Nov 2025; doanhnghiephoinhap 3 Oct 2026 (both secondary) |

- **Not checked:** the full text of Circular 29/2026, SBV safety-ratio circulars (these constrain the lender's balance sheet, not borrower DTI), and any internal SBV guidance. **UNCERTAIN** only for those items.
- **Industry practice:** lenders set their own affordability tests. An educational article by Home Credit Vietnam (21 Oct 2026) says lenders typically look for total debt payments of **33-36% of household income** for mortgages, treats under 36% as best, 43-49% as a warning level and above 50% as critical. It is a general guide, not a rule, and not specific to unsecured consumer loans (https://www.homecredit.vn/blog/ty-le-no-tren-thu-nhap-dti-la-gi-101).
- **What it means for Vay Đúng:**
  - Keep the 40% default in `src/config/affordability.ts` but label it a **"Vay Đúng guideline"**, never "SBV rule" or "bank requirement". Suggested user-facing text: "Ngân hàng Nhà nước không quy định mức trần nợ trên thu nhập; đây là ngưỡng tham khảo của Vay Đúng."
  - Treat the 100 million finance-company cap as an eligibility fact with a date, and the 400 million small-value threshold as a different fact.
  - Do not tell users that a lender will "approve" within a ratio.

## 6. Other rules a borrower-first comparison tool must know

### 6.1 Debt collection

- **Finance companies (Circular 43 art. 7(2)(đ), as amended by 18/2019):** no threats; at most 5 reminders per day; 07:00-21:00 only; no contacting non-debtors (full text read). If a finance company sells debt, the buyer must undertake to follow the same collection rules (art. 10a(10)).
- **Debt-collection as a business:** the Investment Law 2020 added it to the banned businesses, per press (**UNCERTAIN** on the article number). A new Investment Law took effect on 1 Mar 2026; **UNCERTAIN** whether the ban was kept. I did not read either text.
- **What it means for Vay Đúng:** content on "your rights when collectors call" can cite the 5-per-day and 07:00-21:00 rules for finance companies. Do not assert the same limits for banks. I found no equivalent number in Circular 39.

### 6.2 "Tín dụng đen" (black credit) and illegal lending

- No law forbids using the term; officials use it. Related rules: only licensed credit institutions may do banking activity (Law 32/2024 art. 15(2)); the 20%/year civil cap for non-banks (Civil Code art. 468) and the 100%/year criminal line (Criminal Code art. 201, secondary source).
- **Accuracy risk:** calling a *named* app "tín dụng đen" without a basis creates defamation or reputation-protection risk (Civil Code 2015 general provisions on honour and reputation, not re-verified). Use factual language: "Không có trong danh sách tổ chức tín dụng được cấp phép của NHNN (kiểm tra ngày …)". Do not name unlicensed apps as criminals.
- **Licensed list:** keep the dated source in `data/licensed-institutions.json`. Remember that a licensed *parent* company can partner with an unlicensed *app*. Verify who the lender of record is.

### 6.3 Insurance tying and mandatory costs

- Law 32/2024 art. 15(5) (see 1.1). If a lender's terms say insurance is mandatory for approval, store it as a *finding to verify* and show it in the cost with the source.

### 6.4 Fee and price transparency

- Circular 39 art. 16(1) and Circular 43 arts. 10 and 10a oblige lenders to give rate, method and fees before contract. When a lender's page omits these, store `null` and show "chưa rõ"; do not infer.

### 6.5 Other topics I did not research (lawyer or follow-up)

- Business registration and tax for operating the site; e-commerce notification rules for websites; anti-spam rules for email (Decree 91/2020 or successor); the Law on E-commerce 2025; the Consumer Protection Law; IP/trademark use of lender names and logos; Internet service rules for any user-generated content or comments.

## 8. Questions for a Vietnamese lawyer

2. After Law 75/2025 removed the profit test from the definition of advertising (art. 2(1)), is a neutral comparison page "advertising", and does Decree 342/2025 impose any credit-product requirements?
3. Does the Civil Code art. 468 20%/year cap apply to any consumer lender we list (for example a licensed finance company or a bank's partner app)? Confirm the official wording of art. 468 and Criminal Code art. 201 in force now.
4. Is a waitlist email stored with a foreign vendor a cross-border transfer needing the art. 20 dossier, and does the small-business relief (Law art. 38; Decree art. 41) cover it? Which authority receives the dossier and in what form?
5. Is income or debt data typed by a user into a browser-only calculator, and never sent to us, outside the scope of "processing" by us? Is self-declared income "sensitive" under Decree 356 art. 4(1)(k)? Does web analytics fall under art. 4(1)(l)?
6. Does Law art. 27 and Decree 356 art. 8 (finance-sector duties) apply to a comparison tool?

8. Is Circular 43 art. 8a (30% cash cap) and the 100 million cap still fully in force, which parts are "hết hiệu lực một phần", and has the amendment of Oct 2025 been issued?
